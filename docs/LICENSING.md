# Licensing decision

Samsarix Page Lens source code is licensed under the unmodified Mozilla Public License 2.0 (`MPL-2.0`). Copyright notices identify Samsarix LLC as the current project owner.

## Why MPL-2.0

MPL-2.0 is a file-level copyleft license. When someone distributes modified MPL-covered files, those files and their modifications must remain available under MPL. Separate new files can be combined into a larger proprietary or differently licensed work. This gives the extension more protection than a permissive license without imposing the broader whole-program copyleft of GPL-family licenses.

MPL-2.0 also contains contributor patent grants and protects source license notices from removal. It is a standard, OSI-approved license with established tooling and compliance practices.

## Alternatives considered

- **Apache-2.0:** excellent permissive license with patent protection and notice requirements, but downstream distributors are not required to publish modifications. It offers less protection for improvements to this extension.
- **GPL-3.0:** stronger whole-program copyleft, with more integration and adoption friction than this small extension currently needs.
- **AGPL-3.0:** adds obligations for network-interactive software. Samsarix Page Lens has no server component, so the network provision provides little additional practical protection.
- **Business Source License/custom terms:** can reserve commercial rights, but the previous file named a different work, used inconsistent ownership details, and created avoidable review friction. A custom or source-available commercial model should only be introduced with counsel and a concrete business need.

## Credit, ownership, and brand protection

Copyright protects original code and documentation; the `NOTICE`, file headers, and repository history make ownership and attribution visible. MPL requires preservation of substantive license and copyright notices in covered source.

An open-source license does not register or independently protect the Samsarix brand. The license grants no general trademark or logo rights. Samsarix LLC should separately preserve evidence of first use, control official domains/accounts, and consult trademark counsel before relying on or pursuing registration for the product or company name.

Contributors license their contributions under MPL-2.0 through the contribution terms in `CONTRIBUTING.md`. If the project begins accepting material outside contributions or needs proprietary relicensing, Samsarix LLC should consider a signed contributor license agreement or developer certificate of origin after legal review.
